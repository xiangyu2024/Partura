\gap{0.14in}
\key{Auditor's finding, slot E: the PI treated Vwap as the bar's closing price.}
\gap{0.06in}
{\fontsize{18}{22}\selectfont
\begin{blist}
  \item Vwap is the average price traded inside each minute bar, not its close. $\theta$ is its position in the bar (0 = previous close, 1 = close); the PI's $\tfrac12$ is really $\theta$.
  \item PI, with $\theta=1$: $\mathrm{day\_cov}\cdot M/\bar P \approx Q/2 - R\delta$. Auditor: $\approx(\theta-\tfrac12)\,Q - R\delta$.
  \item $\theta$ is per stock (near $\tfrac12$ for active names, near 1 for thin ones), so $(\theta-\tfrac12)$ is a non-linear liquidity trait that linear neutralisation cannot remove (rule \S4, trap 3).
\end{blist}}
\gap{0.06in}
{\notesize\color{sec}Auditor's analysis from the code and the correlation table, not a test. Excerpt, translated and shortened.\\
Source: research/coldstart\_C06/partura/s806/r1/audit\_1.yaml (slot a reasons); counts from audit\_record.json. $M = 241$ bars; $Q$ realised variance; $R$ day return; $\delta$ path offset.\par}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Verdict rule, fixed before any run}
\textfirst
\lead{Primary}\enspace per seed, the heads of an arm are fused (equal-weight z-rank) into one residual book; its net Sharpe on the test segment, $\mathrm{SR}_X(s)$, is paired with the other arm's on the same seed (8 pairs).
\gap{0.22in}
\begingroup\secsize
\renewcommand\arraystretch{1.30}%
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{3.4in}L{8.05in}@{}}
\toprule
\thead{Verdict} & \thead{Condition (one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank, $\alpha = 0.05$)} \\
\midrule
\textcolor{accent}{\lead{Partura is better}} & $p(B > A) < 0.05$ and median of $B - A$ above 0 \\
\lead{Partura is worse} & $p(B < A) < 0.05$ and median of $B - A$ below 0 \\
\lead{Cannot tell} & otherwise \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

% ================================================================== 22 Limitations
\begin{frame}{Known limitations}
\textfirst
\begin{blist}
  \item \lead{Test segment read six times.} C01–C06 each read it; C07 was designed after reading the C05 and C06 test results, and is the seventh read.
  \item \lead{Low power.} Eight seed pairs; the smallest one-sided $p$ is $1/256$. Forward 2026 is only 0.67 years.
  \item \lead{Fixed information schedule.} Round $k$ sees M's first $k$ batches: not true asynchrony. Literature dates are spot-checked only.
  \item \lead{Yardstick unchanged in C06.} The LLM auditor may over- or under-reject. C05's E reward counted the family in the controls, unlike its library rule (fixed in C06). The convex-transform leak stays until C07.
  \item \lead{Survivorship; A-shares, long-only.} The store has no delisted names; T+1 and limit-up names cannot be bought. Both arms share this.
\end{blist}
\end{frame}

% ================================================================== 23 Conclusion
\begin{frame}{What the evidence supports so far}
\textfirst
\begin{nlist}
  \item \lead{No win on the primary metric.} Partura never beat plain GP on its pre-registered metric: two \key{worse} (C01, C02), five \key{cannot tell} (C03–C07).
  \item \lead{The floor held in C05.} With the main search untouched, the fused test book was not worse than A: 0.44 for both.
  \item \lead{One construction survived.} C04's feature, daily return scaled by limit width, came from Partura and survived GP refinement:
    12 of its 14 descendants had positive net excess on test. GP refinement reversed the sign Partura wrote, and the family's forward mean was 0.04.
  \item \lead{Gatekeeping has not helped.} C06's auditor rejects did better out of sample. C07, with a fixed yardstick, is the \key{first} comparison to lean toward the LLM (median +0.11, p 0.062), and its verifier only removed heads.
\end{nlist}
\end{frame}
