\key{Auditor's finding, slot E: the PI treated Vwap as the bar's closing price.}
\gap{0.04in}
{\fontsize{18}{22}\selectfont
\begin{blist}
  \item Vwap is the average price traded inside each minute bar, not its close: $\theta$ is its position in the bar (0 = previous close, 1 = close).

\end{blist}}

% ================================================================== 14 Verdict rule (page 14)
\begin{frame}{Verdict rule, fixed before any run}
\textfirst
\lead{Primary:} per seed, each arm's heads are fused (equal-weight z-rank) into one residual book; its test net Sharpe is paired with A's on the same seed (8 pairs).
\gap{0.18in}
\begingroup\secsize
\renewcommand\arraystretch{1.30}%
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{3.4in}L{8.05in}@{}}
\toprule
\thead{Verdict} & \thead{Condition (one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank, $\alpha = 0.05$)} \\
\midrule
\textcolor{accent}{\lead{Partura is better}} & $p(B > A) < 0.05$ and median of $B - A$ above 0 \\
\lead{Partura is worse} & $p(B < A) < 0.05$ and median of $B - A$ below 0 \\
\lead{Cannot tell} & otherwise \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\endgroup
\gap{0.18in}
\lead{Valid head:} test-segment net excess $> 0$ and $t \ge 2.0$. Heads are de-duplicated greedily on residuals: a candidate with $\rho \ge 0.7$ or $\rho \le -0.5$ to a kept head is dropped.
\gap{0.12in}
{\notesize\color{sec}A is plain GP on the same seeds, with extra evaluations close to Partura's (C05: 4,371 for A, 4,891 for B). The test segment is read once per ledger; no run is repeated because its result looks bad.\\
Reported, never in the verdict: forward 2026, B vs.\ B0, valid heads by arm and mission type. PLAN committed 2026-10-28 (ET), before any run; 14 dated revisions; RESULT §5: none changed a comparison rule.\\
Sources: C05 RESULT header, §2, §5; C06 RESULT §3; scripts/c06\_final.py header (de-duplication).\par}
\end{frame}

% ================================================================== 19 Known limitations (page 19)
\begin{frame}{Known limitations}
\textfirst
\begin{blist}
  \item \lead{Test segment read eight times.} C01–C07 each read it; C08 is the eighth. C08's PLAN calls it a development set; confirmation waits for data after 2026-09-16.
  \item \lead{Low power.} Eight seed pairs; the smallest one-sided $p$ is $1/256$. Forward 2026 is only 0.67 years.
  \item \lead{Fixed information schedule.} Round $k$ sees M's first $k$ batches: not true asynchrony. Literature dates are spot-checked only.
  \item \lead{Yardstick unchanged in C06.} The LLM auditor may over- or under-reject. C05's E reward counted the family in the controls, unlike its library rule (fixed in C06). The convex-transform leak stays until C07.
  \item \lead{Survivorship; A-shares, long-only.} The store has no delisted names; T+1 and limit-up names cannot be bought. Both arms share this.
\end{blist}
\end{frame}
